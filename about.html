About DroneWatch

Transparent tracking of drone incidents across Europe

🎯 Our Mission

DroneWatch provides transparent, real-time information about drone incidents affecting European airspace. We believe the public and journalists deserve access to accurate, verified information about aviation safety and emerging drone-related challenges.

Our platform combines AI-powered news analysis with human verification to create a comprehensive picture of drone activity, making this critical safety information accessible to everyone.

🤖 AI Methodology

How Our AI Works

Our AI system uses state-of-the-art language models to understand and extract information from news articles:

  • AI
    Natural Language Processing
    GPT-4 and Claude models analyze text to identify drone-related incidents with high accuracy
  • AI
    Entity Recognition
    Automatic extraction of locations, times, and impact assessments from unstructured text
  • AI
    Confidence Scoring
    Each discovery includes a transparency score (0-100%) based on source reliability and information completeness
  • AI
    Cross-Reference Validation
    Multiple sources reporting the same incident increase confidence scores

AI Transparency Promise

Every AI-discovered incident is clearly marked with:

  • 🤖 AI badge indicating automated discovery
  • 📊 Confidence percentage for transparency
  • 📰 Direct links to original news sources
  • ✅ Option for human verification

✅ Verification Levels

We use a transparent verification system to help you assess information reliability:

  • Verified
    High Confidence (Score 2-3)
    Multiple reliable sources, official confirmation, or direct evidence
  • Likely
    Medium Confidence (Score 1-2)
    Single reliable source or multiple unconfirmed reports
  • Unverified
    Low Confidence (Score 0-1)
    Single source, social media reports, or preliminary information

⚠️ Important Limitations

Please be aware of these limitations when using DroneWatch:

  • ⏱️
    Not Real-Time
    Updates every 15 minutes means some delay in reporting
  • 📰
    Media Dependent
    We can only report incidents that appear in news sources
  • 🌍
    Coverage Gaps
    Rural areas and smaller incidents may not be captured
  • 🎯
    Not Official
    This is not an official aviation authority service
